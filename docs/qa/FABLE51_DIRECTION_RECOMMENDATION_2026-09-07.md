## Proposed hybrid

### Track 1 — light and shadow, with optional cloth

Keep the restrained grazing opening. From 125 seconds, retain the migrating key and let its cast shadow cross a readable receiving wall. Use the existing detail inserts to examine what the light reveals. Around the existing hush, retain enough wall information that the space persists. At the ending, let the object illumination withdraw before the wall, reversing the opening relationship.

Primary actor: the travelling cast shadow. Additional candidate: a dim, asymmetrical draped form to the side, initially withheld by light and revealed as the key moves. A single baked settling fold near the hush could make this a physical event. Fable explicitly rejects procedural sine-curtain geometry; cloth needs sculpted/simulated folds and has higher production risk.

### Track 4 — the fixture and its shadow

Keep the sodium identity, authored strike/stutter/death timing and zero added fog. Maintain readable fixture/sculpture/wall relationships between detail inserts. Make the failing lamp's effect on a substantial cast shadow visible. Ground or suspend the fixture convincingly.

Fable proposes one principal shadow-casting source to avoid competing shadow edges. Codex checked `chamber-sodium.js`: the existing three-spot fixture already sets `spot.castShadow = isCenter`. Therefore changing the fixture from three shadow casters to one is not a valid task; inspect total scene lighting and improve the existing shadow's visibility and motivation. An optional one-time fixture swing was suggested with an explicit horror-cliche risk. It is not part of the core recommendation and has no owner approval.

### Track 5 — contamination and aftermath

Establish the object within a visible room. Keep the two subtle surface gestures within the existing uninterrupted setup, and preserve the accepted 391.434–432.908-second tar-frontier cut. Return to a recognizable medium composition as the coating advances.

New stage candidate: tar residue spreading onto the floor from the base along the existing monotonic progression, retained through the final return. Its purpose is to show a physical consequence outside the sculpture, not decorate the scene. Pair it with broader reflected highlights that read as wet material. Let a small floor trace outlast the object's illumination. Main risk: a flat decal or another disconnected floor pool.

## Codex qualifications

- Fable sometimes says nothing changes on stage in Tracks 4 and 5. Taken literally that is wrong: the lamp and irreversible tar already change. Its useful criticism is that their effects on the surrounding space are insufficiently developed.
- Afterimage on Track 5 is not literally camera-only: the proposal also has wall/fog development. The visible impact may be small, which is a separate judgment.
- A still every 30 seconds can establish state differences, not prove normal-speed engagement. Any chosen experiment still needs owner playback.
- Chamber's current wall level is a comparison candidate, not a universal numeric exposure prescription.
- A broad highlight requires appropriate reflection/light/material treatment; a larger shadow penumbra alone does not create a broad specular source.
- Fable's proposed light and cloth timing is a creative candidate, not newly heard synchronization. No new audio verification occurred.

No default promotion, cloth adoption, new stage element, camera rewrite, build, deployment or album-transport change is authorized by this reviewer recommendation alone.
