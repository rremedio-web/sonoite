## Why (the problem in numbers)

Today's default Track 4, measured on paused frames: mean brightness **3–6 / 255**; share of the frame brighter than 25 % grey **0.0–0.3 % for the entire film**; change between frames minutes apart **1–4 / 255**. The lamp lights the wall but not the sculpture (both reviewers' #1 point). Nothing happens that a first-time viewer could name.

## The film in one line (kept, sharpened)

**A failing sodium lamp warms up enough to reveal the sculpture and throw its shadow, holds through the song's peak, then loses that reach one object at a time as the music decays, and dies the moment real silence arrives (≈ 386 s).**

## Three rules for this rebuild

1. **States, not curves.** Each state is one sentence about *what is lit*. Changes between states are decisive (a strike, a cut, a reach that clearly arrives), not multi-minute ramps.
2. **One ratchet, two directions.** The lamp's **reach** (how far its light gets: tube → flower rim → hand → wall shadow → room) only **grows** while it warms (0 → peak) and only **shrinks** after the peak. It never returns to an earlier state. Whatever is lost stays lost: that is the lasting mark. This is a pure function of track time, so it is seek-safe like the tar.
3. **Visible or it didn't happen (measured).** Consecutive states must differ by **≥ 20 / 255** in the sculpture's region, or by a different thing being lit. The sculpture's lit area must exceed **5 %** of frame at peak (today: 0.3 %). Visibility comes from **contrast** (edges, rims, one hard shadow), not global exposure, so the darkness stays.

Kept from the current film: the single sodium light species, every authored strike / stutter / sputter / death time, zero fog, static camera holds plus the BURN push, no camera or sculpture reaction to live audio, dim amber rather than orange wash, the grey mesh.

## Optional (your call): a mark that outlives the lamp

On the AFTER wide, a **faint afterimage of the flower's shadow stays on the wall** for a few seconds after the light that cast it is gone (S5/S6). This reads as the eye's retinal persistence, and the existing dark-adaptation ("scotopic") system could carry it. It is the one element that adds something rather than only taking away. Risk: it reads as a rendering glitch. Off by default; only built as a separate arm.
