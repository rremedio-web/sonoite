7 September 2026. Owner requested a deep dive through the Markdown ideas, looking beyond cloth for lighting, effects and additional stage elements. During the review, the owner also authorized a trial of the T1 shadow / T4 fixture / T5 aftermath hybrid. Research below includes a larger reserve; it is not a mandate to implement that reserve.

## Main finding

**The archive contains a richer stage vocabulary than the current films use. The problem is not a shortage of ideas; it is how often an idea was reduced to a tiny opacity change or forbidden by a blanket restraint rule.**

The March reset explicitly says the room is an instrument and breathing is texture rather than structure. Yet its surrounding specifications repeatedly require the room to have no hard edges, no independent silhouette, no second object, almost no atmosphere, and changes below conscious perception. That leaves little for a long held shot to contain. The subsequent response is often a camera re-score. The owner's current objection follows directly from this contradiction.

The early cover-art study is unusually revealing: it diagnoses the model-viewer trap, but also treats any additional legible silhouette as a failure. That safeguard addressed a particular dual-hero composition. Applied universally, it prevents the stage from doing meaningful work. A clear hierarchy is useful; a ban on every supporting form is not.

## What the artwork actually gives us

Two different visual relationships: a pale, folded covering over an ambiguous body-like volume; and a dark hand presenting pale organic forms and a thin metal element. Its strength is in weight, tension, contrast, contact and partial concealment. Neither panel establishes a literal cathedral, science lab, showroom, cosmic void or fantasy altar.

Translate those relationships into the stage. Do not assume the metal element definitively establishes a measurement/caliper narrative. Do not replace the retained grey sculpture with the dark material study the owner found unreadable. Cloth is one route to the left panel's physical tension; it is not the only route to atmosphere.

## Beyond cloth: eight useful candidates

These are alternatives to compare, not layers to stack.

| Candidate | What the listener would see | Best fit | Production/quality condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Travelling cast shadow** | The sculpture stays still while its shadow becomes larger, shifts across the room and remains changed on return | T1, authorized trial | Light and receiving surface must be readable. No extra shadow painted onto a plane. |
| **Weighted fold or membrane** | A partly concealed form with one loaded fold, a weighted edge and perhaps one settling movement | T1 later; T2 future | Sculpted/baked physical shape, no periodic sine curtain. Dim does not mean blurred. |
| **Unseen occluder** | Something outside the frame briefly interrupts the light across both object and room | T1/T2 candidate | A coherent shadow crossing, not a generic dark wipe. Avoid implying a literal monster unless wanted. |
| **Narrow opening and reflected light** | A crease or boundary admits a thin patch of illumination; the spill changes what can be read nearby | T1/T2/T3 candidate | The opening needs a real surface relationship; do not make another glowing line graphic. |
| **Fixture plus affected space** | The lamp's attempts to stay lit alternately reveal and lose a substantial shadow and its support | T4, authorized trial | Visible source position and illumination agree. Retain zero fog and current timing. |
| **Contact and wet aftermath** | Coating reaches beyond the sculpture, leaving a dark, reflective trace that persists | T5, authorized trial | Must read as matter attached to the base, not a light pool or flat decal. |
| **Focus exchange** | Attention shifts between a nearby physical edge and the sculpture without moving the camera | Later, only with two useful depth planes | Real surface information first. DOF cannot rehabilitate blurry primitive props. |
| **Light withdrawal in layers** | Direct illumination disappears, then the wall/reflection, leaving a final trace | T1/T5, authorized trial | Separate envelopes, readable intermediate states, no premature black over audible outro. |

The archive's HoloCloth cavity study is useful as material research, not a reason to add rainbow holography. Cymatics is a separate future-track proposal, not an excuse to restore a dotted floor. Refractive glass, ribbons, micro-strands, procedural particles and ray tracing remain higher-cost reserves without an established need in these three films.

## Mood and attention over a whole track

Track 1 should discover rather than display: restrained entrance, a changing relationship between object and shadow, then withdrawal. Its room can gradually acquire a boundary or concealed fold. Track 4 should endure: the fixture and what it can illuminate are the changing subject, while the sculpture gives the light a surface to act on. Track 5 should accumulate: the existing material change has consequences beyond a few highlights on the head.

Those are staging hypotheses grounded in the owner-approved trial, not newly heard interpretations of the recordings. A still sequence can show that the state changes. It cannot establish that the timing feels musical or that a two-minute hold is compelling. Normal-speed owner listening remains the artistic test.

For each held passage ask: what is readable, what changes, what remains changed, and why does the next view matter? If the only answer is a different camera position, the stage is still under-authored. If everything changes together, it becomes difficult to perceive any one cause.

## Experience findings beyond the stage

- **Playback should be understandable.** Clear play/pause, seeking, track choice and replay matter more than a navigable 3D lobby. The old archive repeatedly reaches this conclusion; its claims that the prototype has no track selector are stale.
- **The interface should recede and reliably return.** Dim controls must remain discoverable and usable by keyboard/touch. Avoid turning navigation into obscure in-world symbols or audio-reactive labels.
- **Comparison needs to retain context.** Passage shortcuts help inspection, but full-track entry/development/ending matters. A fixed-camera toggle can isolate lighting without pretending it is the finished edit.
- **The album-flow decision is still open.** Some March docs demand a black gap; Gemini later proposes both hard cuts and continuity. Neither is owner approval to interrupt or crossfade the music. Title-card-per-track versus continuous listening needs its own concrete transport comparison.
- **A hidden tab is an experience condition.** The known picture/audio behavior must be described honestly during reviews; diagnostic seeks and uninterrupted visible viewing are different evidence.
- **Accessibility is part of mood.** Reduced motion should retain meaningful state changes, and pause/seek must restore consistent scenes. Do not make “nothing responds” the reduced-motion interpretation by accident.

## Decision ledger

**Retain:** grey sculpture; neutral T1 emergence; T4 sodium and current strike/death timing; T5 monotonic tar and accepted 391.434–432.908-second closer; subtle two-cue surface behavior; readable form; no camera beat-bouncing.

**Rejected by recent owner feedback:** candle-and-orange-pool look, dotted dust lattices, blurry balls and primitive-looking haze/cylinder surroundings; dark split-material treatment that loses the sculpture. These reject particular visual results, not all atmosphere or supporting elements.

**Authorized now:** a separate T1 shadow / T4 mounted fixture / T5 floor aftermath trial, with close details and recurring compositions. Existing proposals remain selectable.

**Research reserve:** better cloth, an unseen occluder, a real aperture/light boundary, focus exchange, later-track-specific matter. No implicit adoption, no full-album rewrite.

## Recommended sequence

1. Make the authorized hybrid reviewable, with actual captures, backward-seek checks and normal-speed passages.
2. Judge T1 shadow, T4 fixture/shadow and T5 wet trace independently. A positive verdict on one does not approve all three.
3. Use the strongest extra-stage candidate for the remaining weakness. If T1 still lacks presence, compare a weighted fold against an off-screen occlusion. If T5 residue looks flat, improve the light/material relationship before adding more matter.
4. Only then extend the chosen vocabulary to the other four tracks and address album transport separately.

The archive supports a more active, physically specific stage. It does not support restoring every discarded effect, making every track the same room with different color, or reducing the next step to cloth alone.
