## Fixes proposed (Fable's specifics; Astra compatible)

- **Break the palindrome.** On the way down, keep the cone wide and let the level fall, so far things drop out first and the picture *dims* instead of rewinding. Let the grey mesh in darkness survive between AFTER restrikes. Or build the optional wall afterimage.
- **Make the shadow real.** At 285.5 (arm F), land a **hard flower shadow** on the in-frame wall: a narrower, harder cone at full reach, not a wider, softer one. "The lamp finally throws a shadow" can be named in a close-up.
- **Let the existing 319.10 wide show the whole room.** Today the first loss fires at 320.5, 1.4 s after that cut. Move the four losses to later sputters (323 / 330 / 333.5 / 336) so the wide holds the full room for about 4 s. Then every loss is of something the viewer just saw.
- **Colour:** neutral charcoal plaster, saturation ≤ 0.35, wall at most about ¼ of the flower's lit side. Desaturate the key on the mesh to about 0.25–0.30. **The tube should be the only saturated thing in frame.**
- **Replace the pin-spot** with a wide cone and distance falloff, so nearness to the tube orders the reveal: crown → fingers → egg. That removes the need for the 3.5× boost. At minimum, halve it on the ascent and remove it on the descent.
- **S2:** land the wall and shadow as a *step* at the 187 cut, not a 117 s ease.
- **Measure 269.86 before the F/A playback.** It is a Gemini-only event.
