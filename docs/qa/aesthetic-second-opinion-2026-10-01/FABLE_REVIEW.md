# Sonoite — second opinion (Fable seat, 2026-10-01)

Scope: the Path B default route (what `/` and `?track=` serve) unless a flag is named. Judged from stills and source only; nothing was heard.

## Verdict

The three films share a credible grammar (grey sculpture, darkness, located light) and each has one image worth the visit: T1's flower at 384 s, T4's lamp outliving the song, T5's tar hand at 478 s. Between those images the default route is under-authored: light steps too small to see, long holds on near-black wides, and structural cuts sitting seconds off the measured hits. Since 7 September the real gains are the quiet scene and the listening map, but the best later ideas (travelling shadow, tar-frontier close-up, lamp-as-key) are all still behind flags, so the album reads as one room in three colours.
