# Track 1 — return to the references

8 September 2026. Owner rejected the new background, asked for a darker reliquary focus, subtle differentiated musical responses, and a return to the Sleep Token / external project references. These directions supersede the earlier decision to hold the opening camera until 125 seconds.

## Finding

The current trial is a brightly described sculpture in front of a large, visibly textured panel. It needs a dim subject with concentrated highlights, depth between surfaces, and meaningful development. Broad background coverage and added surface noise pushed the scene away from both the cover and the generated hanging-fold concept. This is a staging and asset-quality problem, not something another gain adjustment alone can settle.

The reference lesson is selective disclosure: show a contour, its support and the light's reach; let a later view reveal new material or spatial information. The owner wants an active musical environment. Restraint must not become a rule that suppresses all visible development.

Retain the current recording landmarks while changing which part of the sculpture is legible. Frame the cast shadow as a spatial relationship, not a permanent large black graphic on a bright panel.

### UVA — Our Time 1x3

[Creator documentation](https://www.uva.co.uk/features/our-time-2023) describes moving pendulums that project light and sound through the installation. In the saved film sample, separate beams and their landing points make source position, distance and people in the room readable.

**Borrow:** one source's motion produces consequences in several places. **Avoid:** literal pendulums, a line of identical spotlights or copying its floor pools. **Track 1 consequence:** music can slightly change the travelling light's reach, with the real shadow and receiving surfaces following. ### Radiohead — KID A MNESIA

The [creator interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/spotlights/creating-kid-a-mnesia-an-exclusive-look-at-radiohead-s-new-virtual-exhibit?lang=en) describes artwork-led rooms and a shrine-like lighting approach. **Borrow:** the album's artwork determines material, concealment and spatial relationships. **Avoid:** importing its museum architecture, paper room or particle paintings literally. **Track 1 consequence:** the cover's cloth suggests weight over something concealed. ### Max Cooper — Unspoken Words / Growth

The [Growth chapter](https://unspokenwords.net/growth/) connects a central musical pulse with bodily expansion and spatial treatment. That is a specific musical/visual relationship, not a claim that every object uses the same amplitude signal.

**Borrow:** choose a physical response that expresses the role of the sound. **Avoid:** borrowing its anatomy, animation style, or attaching an unapproved literal narrative to Sonoite. **Track 1 consequence:** assign each element a different response and settling time. Separate phrase-scale development from small continuous modulation.

### Opera North / Lusion — The Turn of the Screw

[Creator documentation](https://lusion.co/projects/the_turn_of_the_screw/) describes an audiovisual environment built around spatial recordings. The saved browser samples retain foreground/background relationships through different atmospheric states.

**Borrow:** continuous space whose conditions change, with a passive viewing path. **Avoid:** the landscape, bright motes and coloured wash. **Track 1 consequence:** retain distinct near, subject and far depth planes. Haze should reveal a gap between them, not flatten them into a grey screen.

## Revised Track 1 visual target

**Relic first:** dark body, readable contour, occasional restrained highlights on petals/egg/metal. Keep the preferred grey mesh. Adjust source angle, area and light balance before altering the whole-film exposure.

**Background:** withdraw the large coarse-textured panel. A supporting cloth form needs actual weight, tension and contact. The next asset comparison should be a shaped/baked cloth object with broad folds and restrained micro-detail, positioned deeper and partly outside the composition. It must pass a plain-light close/mid/wide check before any music modulation is added. The generated concept is a target for construction and light hierarchy, not proof the current procedural mesh matches it.

**Atmosphere:** a restrained depth interval touched by the key/backlight. Preserve deep black away from the source. No arbitrary black-percentage quota; a dim fold still needs enough local contrast to communicate its shape.

**Camera:** restore opening progression and distinct later inserts. Hold when light, concealment or material changes offer something to observe. The diagnostic lock remains available but defaults off. Do not solve every weak hold with another camera cut.

## Each element's musical role

These are implementation targets following the owner's latest direction. They are **not implemented or newly validated by listening** in this reference pass.

| Element | Subtle musical behaviour | Character and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relic surface | Local roughness/sheen change from sustained midrange energy | Faster than the environment, small highlight movement, no geometry shaking or whole-body flash |
| Travelling key | Restrained reach/intensity modulation from smoothed low-frequency energy inside its authored path | Few-second attack/recovery; phrase changes retain priority; no one-second detector metronome |
| Cloth | Small pressure/relaxation in a weighted fold from a slower low-frequency envelope | Longer response and settling; grounded hem; no perpetual procedural wind loop |
| Shadow | Responds to the real light and cloth movement | Physically coupled shape/displacement; no independent fake shadow pulse |
| Haze | Very slow change in the lit depth interval from sustained overall energy | Slower than the key; preserves black and silhouette; no distinct sprites |
| Floor/contact | Small reflected-light changes caused by the key; persistent contact shadow | Subordinate, no candle/orange pool, no independent luminous decal |
| Camera | Authored changes in what can be seen at structural landmarks | Phrasing rather than bass-following bounce; short settling/reframing only when it adds information |

The implementation currently takes `elapsed` and `state.started` into `sampleProposalLight`, then overwrites the key/rim/wall/fog values in `updateProposalLighting`. That final Stage override does not consume audio analysis. Existing shared onset activity therefore cannot establish this proposed musical relationship. Reuse the analyser and its reset/seek lifecycle, but give the Stage composition bounded inputs with distinct response times. No new generic beat detector is called for.

Paused/seeked states, silence, reduced motion and track navigation must remain consistent. The owner has requested subtle reactivity, not mandatory motion during actual silence. Musical cue validity still needs the recording and owner audition.
